Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you play Neon Drop on this website, and what choices you have. We have deliberately built the game to need as little data as possible.

1. Information the game collects

Neon Drop has no accounts, no login and no sign-up form. The game itself does not ask you for, transmit or store your name, email address, phone number or payment details.

Your high score and in-game coin balance are written to your browser's local storage. That data never leaves your device and is not readable by us. Clearing your browser's site data removes it permanently.

2. Information collected automatically

  • Server logs. Our hosting provider may record standard request data such as IP address, browser type, referring page and timestamp, for security and reliability purposes.
  • Analytics. If privacy-friendly analytics are enabled, they record aggregate page views and country-level location without cookies or cross-site tracking.

3. Advertising and cookies

This site is free because it is supported by advertising. Advertising on this site is served by Adsterra and its advertising partners. When advertising is enabled, those partners may set cookies or similar identifiers on your device in order to deliver ads, measure them, and detect fraudulent traffic.

Advertising is off until you agree to it. On your first visit no advertising script is loaded at all — the page and the game work fully without it. A banner asks whether you accept advertising cookies, and nothing third-party is requested unless you choose Accept. Your choice is remembered on this device. To change it, clear this site's data in your browser settings; the banner will appear again on your next visit.

  • If you accept, Adsterra and its partners may use cookies to serve and measure ads, including ads based on your visits to this and other websites.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising from many vendors at aboutads.info/choices, or from Google at Google Ads Settings.

When Neon Drop is played on a third-party game portal, that portal's own advertising and consent systems apply instead of this one.

4. Children

Neon Drop is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Where the game is distributed on portals with child audiences, advertising is requested in a non-personalised, child-directed treatment mode.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data is shared only with the hosting provider, analytics provider and advertising partners described above, strictly to operate the site.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, and to object to processing or lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Because we hold almost no identifiable data, most requests can be satisfied simply by clearing your browser storage. To make a formal request, contact us at the address below.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected in the "last updated" date at the top of this page.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: see our contact page.
