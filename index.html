How to play

Sixty seconds to learn, weeks to master. There is no timer — only the board.

  1. Aim. Slide your finger or move the mouse across the board to choose a column. A glowing guide shows exactly where the tile will land.
  2. Drop. Tap, click, or hit Space. Gravity does the rest.
  3. Merge. Two or more identical tiles touching side-by-side or stacked fuse into one tile of double the value. Merge three or four at once and the value doubles again for each extra tile.
  4. Cascade. After a merge everything falls and re-checks. Set up chains and your combo multiplier climbs with every link.
  5. Survive. The run ends when the board is full and no merges remain. Beat your best score, then beat it again.

Frequently asked questions

Is Neon Drop really free?

Yes. The whole game is free to play with no paywall and no account. It is supported by optional ads.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs directly in any modern browser — Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge — on phone, tablet, laptop or desktop.

Does it work offline?

Once the page has loaded, the game logic runs entirely on your device. Only the ads need a connection.

Where is my score saved?

In your browser's local storage, on your device only. Clearing site data will reset it.

How is the score calculated?

Each merge awards points equal to the new tile's value, multiplied up by your current combo chain. Bigger cascades are worth dramatically more than the same merges made separately.
